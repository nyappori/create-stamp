# 60 Poster Pose Design Table

各動作ごとの決めポーズ設計メモです。
## 01 big_jump
- 向き: front
- 感情: joyful excited
- 決めポーズ: airborne jump peak
- 要約: The duck is at the highest point of a joyful jump, both feet off the ground, both wings raised high, mouth open in a big smile, body slightly stretched upward.
- 補足: Keep the full body centered. This is the static sticker poster pose for jumping.
- 画像名想定: `01_big_jump_pose.png`
