# Sample copy-paste prompts

## 01 big jump
```text
Keep exactly the same duck character and the same plain green background as the input image.

One duck only. Full body always visible.
Keep the duck's design consistent at all times: round white body, orange beak and feet, small black eyes, light pink cheeks, three small feathers on top of the head, short rounded wings, thick black outline, cute soft proportions.

Keep the camera completely fixed.
Do not change the background.
Do not change the duck design.
Do not add any other characters, objects, text, shadows, scenery, or effects.
Do not add extra wings, legs, eyes, or feathers.
Use a cute, readable animation that is easy to understand without text.

Front view only. The duck crouches down clearly and deeply. Then it makes one very high joyful vertical jump. Both feet clearly leave the ground. Both wings rise high during the jump. At the top of the jump, the duck looks very happy. Then the duck lands on both feet, the body compresses slightly, makes one cute soft bounce, and returns to the same front-facing standing pose as at the beginning.
```
